# How to pay for foreign services from Russia (2026)

A maintained list of 137 foreign services (AI tools, games, streaming, developer and work tools, travel) and the ways
people in Russia pay for them in 2026. Cards issued by Russian banks (Visa, Mastercard, Mir) are not accepted by these
services, so every entry lists what works instead and links to a step-by-step guide.

Data: [`data/services.csv`](data/services.csv), [`data/services.json`](data/services.json) (CC BY 4.0).
Live USDT/RUB rate and card terms: [open data](https://saikyo.exchange/en/open-data), JSON API `https://saikyo.exchange/api/public/usdt-rub`, MCP server `https://saikyo.exchange/api/mcp`.
Русская версия: [README.ru.md](README.ru.md).

## Payment options in general

1. **Foreign virtual card.** A Visa card issued abroad and topped up with rubles. Example: the Omega Wallet virtual Visa
   offered by [SAIKYO](https://saikyo.exchange/en/card): 1,990 RUB one-time, 0 RUB maintenance, top-up via SBP. Other providers are compared in
   [this table](https://saikyo.exchange/en/foreign-card-comparison). Merchant acceptance varies, so test with a small payment first.
2. **Gift card or ready subscription paid in rubles**, where the service sells codes or allows activation on your account.
3. **Account region.** Services marked "restricts Russian accounts" also need the account region and billing country to be
   a supported country. A card alone is not enough.

Before paying, check the service terms for your country. This list is informational; it is not legal or financial advice.
